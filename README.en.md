# Inkling

*Worth noting.*

A personal vocabulary journal, built around words, notes, and the connections between them.

**[Open Inkling](https://evelyn-y-chen.github.io/gre-quick-recall/)** · **[中文版](README.md)** · **[Suggestions & issues](https://github.com/evelyn-y-chen/gre-quick-recall/issues)**

No registration is needed. Choose a few words, try to recall them, and use red, yellow, or green to record how familiar they feel today. When you want to check your recall more closely, start a test.

## Why I made Inkling

I wanted a vocabulary tool that felt easier to begin using.

Many study apps start with a test. For me, the fear of getting things wrong could turn into anxiety before I had even begun, and sometimes I would avoid opening the app altogether. Inkling puts a simple red–yellow–green review mode first: record what you remember today, start whenever you feel ready, and stop whenever you need to. Tests are still available when you want to check your memory.

I also wanted somewhere to keep my own notes. Few vocabulary apps I had tried let me freely record and revise a memory method for each word. A teacher's explanation, a connection that suddenly makes sense, or a distinction between confusing words can disappear if I do not write it down. I wanted a small space beside each word where those thoughts could stay—and change as I learned.

This began as something for myself. I am sharing it free of charge in the hope that it may help others too. Suggestions are welcome through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/evelyn-y-chen/gre-quick-recall/issues); I will improve what I can, gradually. The project is still evolving. You do not need to use every feature—start with the approach that works for you.

## Behind the Name · 名字的由来

### Inkling · A small beginning

An *inkling* is a slight idea or a first hint of understanding. Learning a word can begin with a faint impression or a connection that starts to make sense. Inkling is a place to keep those small insights and build on them.

The *ink* in the name also brings writing to mind: a space beside each word for thoughts of your own.

### Worth noting.

To note is both to notice and to write something down.

*In the margins, something of your own.*

在词与词之间，留下自己的注解。

## Getting started

On your first visit, **nine illustrated feature cards** explain the core functions. Swipe, use Back / Next or the arrow keys, and switch between Chinese and English. Close anytime with **×**; it will not reopen automatically in the same browser. Reopen it through **使用指南 → 翻看功能介绍** (Guide → Illustrated introduction). A separate dismissible tip explains home-screen installation for iPhone, iPad, Android, Chrome and Mac Safari. Installation does not automatically move guest records; sign in or export a learning note first.


1. Choose account sync (username/password without email, or an available social sign-in) or start without an account in learning-note mode. Then choose a palette and optional nickname for your backup filename.
2. Select **开始刷词** (Start reviewing), choose a list and a small range, then review in order or shuffle.
3. Recall the meaning before revealing it. Mark your confidence with red, yellow, or green, and edit a word's note when useful.
4. Use **开始测试** (Start test) when you want to check your recall. For a daily schedule, create a plan under **今天的学习** (Daily plan) on the home page.
5. Submit edits with their Save buttons. **已存到这台设备** (Saved on this device) confirms they have reached browser storage. Export a learning note periodically.

The **使用指南** (Guide) is always available at the top. Select the **Inkling** wordmark to return to the start screen. **关于 Inkling** (About Inkling) explains the name and the idea behind the project. The interface is currently in Chinese; this guide includes its key labels.

## Red, yellow, green: record how it feels today

| Mark | Meaning | A useful next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Red — unfamiliar / not recalled | You cannot recall it yet | Bring it back for focused review |
| Yellow — uncertain / partly recalled | Familiar, but not clear | Review it and note the confusing part |
| Green — familiar / clear | You can actively recall it | Move on and check it again later |

These are self-assessments for this review. You can change them at any time, and you do not have to get everything right. Filter review screens by familiarity; some allow several colors at once. Mark-saving controls save to your own current workspace.

**The vocabulary notebook uses the same system.** Review your collection in compact rows, reveal definitions and notes, and rate each word. Keyboard navigation scrolls the active notebook word toward the middle of the screen.

## Study checklist: one table for every round

**Today’s small goals**

Open Study checklist on the home page or in the header. Select banks and Lists, new learning or review, and self-checks or supported practice. Dark chips with ✓ are selected. Check off completed Lists yourself or undo them. Unfinished goals remain visible the next day, without a long-term deadline.

**One table for every round**

The master table uses original Lists or categories: round 1 is initial study, later rounds are reviews. Choose how many rounds to display. Daily goals share these completion records. Check or undo a round, or select its date to correct it. Add past study using an actual date, round and several Lists, even if you had not opened Inkling that day.

**Seven days from actual study**

Lists enter the review view after being checked off or backdated in the master table. The chart shows their next review from real progress. Overdue work appears today; later reviews are calculated after actual completion, without assuming future correct answers. Red suggests tomorrow, yellow two days, green three days, then 7, 15 and 30 after actual due reviews. Manual completion without a color initially suggests three days. These are suggestions, not forgetting probabilities or mandatory tasks. Today’s new-study count uses completed first rounds; future new learning is not guessed. Due words are deduplicated across banks.

**Keep your earlier records**

Checking a round never assigns green ratings. Undoing it does not delete actual self-checks, notes or traffic lights. Earlier manual completions remain readable; old future reservations no longer run or count as completions. Undated old ratings are not assigned invented dates; add past study yourself. Checklists and dates travel with your account or a full backup. Ratings-only merge does not import a whole checklist.

Practice can combine Lists and any selection of red, yellow, green and unrated entries. Selecting no colors yields no test entries. General vocabulary, A+ and BB support multiple-choice practice; other banks use their existing self-checks.

## Your vocabulary notebook

- **Record context first.** Create a collection with a source and date to find the original article, question, or lesson later.
- **Use one search box.** Search all modules, filter by list, or enter and edit a new word yourself.
- **Choose several definitions.** Select several sources for the same word. Within a collection, they combine under one entry with their source information retained.
- **Set your source order.** Move sources up or down under **词库来源优先顺序**; results follow your preference.
- **Use bilingual dictionary meanings.** If there is no list match, the dictionary/manual area opens using the word already typed. Choose ECDICT meanings to insert, then revise them.
- **Look up words in Merriam-Webster and Dict.cn.** When adding a word, expand “查词典或补充这一个词”; for an existing entry, expand “补查词典”. Select “韦氏词典” (Merriam-Webster) or “海词词典” (Dict.cn) to open the word’s official entry in a new window, then return to keep editing. External definitions are not automatically imported and do not overwrite notes.
- **Switch editing and review modes.** Edit words, definitions, and sources; review in compact rows with hidden meanings and notes until revealed. Subdued source labels keep definitions clear.
- **Use marks and shortcuts.** Rate words and navigate with arrows, Enter, Space, and 1 / 2 / 3. An on-screen undo restores an accidentally removed word.

Save notebook edits with **保存到当前工作区** (Save to current workspace). Shared memory edits use **保存全部笔记** (Save all notes).

## One word, one personal memory note

Default memory methods were deduplicated and shortened for review; they are not verbatim transcripts. Private edits saved on the creator's personal website have not been imported as public defaults.

### Study videos and note sources

- **Study alongside a video:** some public guided memorization or practice videos for A+ and GLEE 1500 are available on related websites or Bilibili. Search by the course or word-list name, with terms such as “带背” or “带练”.
- **Video-based notes:** most default notes were compiled from public A+, GLEE and other teaching videos, then deduplicated and shortened for review and your own annotations.
- **BB memory methods:** these come from the three original PDF volumes, not video notes.
- **Other word lists:** General vocabulary and Equivalent pairs use lists from 青山学堂 (Qingshan Xuetang). Cloze high-frequency 3000 was compiled from online resources.
- **AI-organized notes:** GLEE default notes from **List 22 onward, including List 22**, were organized using AI. The teacher's videos had not yet covered those lists when the notes were compiled, so this part is not based on corresponding video explanations.

Where memory notes are supported, the same word uses one personal note across modules. Edit it wherever you encounter it; after saving, the update appears elsewhere too. Edit several words, then use **保存全部笔记 / 保存全部记忆方法** (Save all notes / memory methods) to save together.

“Shared” means **between your own modules**, not published to other learners. Separate browser profiles keep independent edits; one person's changes do not rewrite the default lists for everyone.

## Tests, when you want them

- **A+** keeps its original equivalents test under the Scan/notes–Start test switch. Choose a meaning, then synonyms. A wrong or uncertain first step shows answers and memory notes, skipping step two. Original single/select-all choices, retries and scheduled reviews remain.
- **BB** has one simple six-choice synonym test. Set a range and order, answer directly, then see the correct synonym, meanings and memory notes.
- **General vocabulary** keeps one Start test entry with range, direction and order settings, plus notes after mistakes.

Saved legacy test records are retained. The study checklist can open the original bank for self-checks, use practice where available, or be checked off manually. Tests can be saved and resumed; long feedback and notes scroll within their own areas.

## Saving, backups, and moving devices

### Save without signing in

Saved notes, familiarity marks, tests/review history, study checklists and word records, source order, nickname, and palette are written automatically to the current browser's IndexedDB. Return with the **same website address and browser profile** to continue. Submit editor changes first, then check for **已存到这台设备** (Saved on this device).

Devices and browser profiles have separate data; people sharing one profile share its workspace. Private browsing, clearing site data, or browser cleanup can remove local records. Revision checks help prevent stale tabs from overwriting new records, but editing in one tab is recommended.

### Take a learning note with you

1. Save edits and select **带走便笺 / 导出我的便笺** (Export my learning note).
2. Download a JSON file named with your nickname and local date/time, such as `Inkling_小词_便笺_2026-10-14_153538.json`. Without a nickname, it uses 我的 (“mine”).
3. Open Inkling on the new device, choose **读取便笺 / 我有一张便笺** (Import a learning note), select the file, and confirm.
4. Saved records and palette return, followed by a brief confetti message.

Choose **Merge ratings** to combine different-word ratings while keeping your current notes, notebook, tests and theme. For the same word, the newer recorded edit wins; ambiguous older records require review. **Replace all records** restores the entire file and overwrites the destination workspace, so export current records first. Legacy GRE JSON backups are supported. Slips are ordinary, unencrypted files; keep them private. Unsaved drafts are not guaranteed to be restored.

Guests do not need an email or password. A learning note carries its data independently of an account.

Reload the latest account progress on the other device to view the merged result.

Records include dates, features, counts and technical identifiers, not note or backup contents. Aggregates support improvements and project, portfolio or research presentations; data is not sold or used for advertising or other commercial purposes. ## Account or learning-note mode

Choose GitHub sign-in (the first sign-in creates your Inkling account) for account sync, or use directly without registering and export/import learning-note files. The in-app guide has Chinese and English instructions for all modules, notebook collection and review, traffic-light self-checks, notes, tests, scheduled reviews, shortcuts, backups, themes and feedback.

Feedback is available to guests and account users. Guest submissions are public; signed-in users can choose Public or Private, defaulting to Private. Everyone can read public suggestions and replies; private feedback stays between its submitting account and the author. Existing messages remain private. The author replies from Statistics → User feedback, and users refresh Feedback to check; no notification emails are sent. Analytics supports 7, 30, 90 days and All time, with active visitors, review actions and new accounts for the selected range.

In two-step equivalent-word tests (A+ and BB), an incorrect or uncertain meaning now goes directly to feedback with the correct meaning, equivalents and notes. Synonym selection is skipped; unattempted synonyms are not counted as missed. Retry and mastery rules remain in effect.

My progress shows practiced words, self-rated familiar words, completed rounds and traffic-light progress bars for the three modules with the most recorded practice. Words are deduplicated; conflicting ratings use the less-familiar state. Completion history starts with this version, supplemented by identifiable saved older rounds. Records travel with the account or learning-note file.

Focused test panels keep Submit/Next visible on phones, tablets and computers. Long explanations and notes scroll inside their own areas at readable font sizes.

Recovery and undo: imports, replacements and loading a different copy first save a full device checkpoint (the latest two distinct historical copies). Undo the last import/replacement from More in the header, or use the read-only device recovery page to export retained copies. Checkpoints stay in the current browser/web app and cannot recreate unsaved history from before this update.

Save status: device writes run independently of cloud uploads. “Sync now” shows progress, checks the account, and reports a confirmation time and revision, or an explicit failure/timeout.

Device storage: each workspace retains at most two distinct historical recovery copies, rotating out older ones. Ordinary editing checkpoints at most once per 15 minutes; imports and replacements checkpoint immediately. Current records and required pending sync copies remain separate. Identical contents are not repeatedly backed up. Updates retain only the current program cache, removing old assets without deleting study records. Manually exported learning notes remain under your control.
